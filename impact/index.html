How outcomes are measured

The grade improvement figure is not an estimate — it comes from logging each student's grades before a term begins and again after it ends, and writing that change directly into the outcome number. Growth in the program is measured the same honest way: the free weekly schedule stays fixed, so the student count climbs only as far as the team can genuinely support it.
