Free science tutoring for deaf & hard-of-hearing students in Gujranwala

Tareeqa partners with Bibi 'N' Baba School in Gujranwala to teach chemistry and planting through observation and hands-on work instead of rote memorization. Every lesson is built to be watched and done, since many of our students can't rely on a spoken explanation.

How it works See the impact

What we do

Visual, hands-on lessons

Simple chemistry experiments and planting activities built around observation and doing, since lecture-style teaching doesn't reach deaf and hard-of-hearing students the same way.

No handed-over answers

Lessons are built around small groups reasoning through problems together, so understanding sticks instead of being memorized.

A team, not a solo act

I recruit and direct other tutors, and we run a fixed free weekly schedule so growth stays tied to what the team can actually deliver.
