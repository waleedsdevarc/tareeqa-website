Free science tutoring for deaf & hard-of-hearing students in Gujranwala

Tareeqa partners with a deaf school to teach chemistry and planting through observation and hands-on work — not rote memorization. Lessons are built to be seen and done, not heard.

What we do

Visual, hands-on lessons

Simple chemistry experiments and planting activities designed for how deaf students learn — through observation and doing, not lecture.

No handed-over answers

Every lesson is built so small groups reason through problems together, so understanding is built rather than memorized.

A team, not a solo act

We recruit and direct other tutors, running a fixed free weekly schedule so the program grows on a real, honest footing.
