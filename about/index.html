About Tareeqa

The story behind the program, who it serves, and how each lesson is designed.

The founding idea

Tareeqa started in June 2023 as a free science tutoring effort for deaf and hard-of-hearing students in Gujranwala. It was founded and is led by Waleed Ahmed Syed. We partnered directly with Bibi 'N' Baba School, an inclusive school open to all students, so the students we teach through its deaf and hard-of-hearing unit are genuinely deaf and hard-of-hearing rather than a general classroom. That direct partnership is why the program is built around what these students actually need, instead of guesses about it.

How lessons are designed

Visual by design

Every lesson is visual and hands-on. Instead of a lecture, students work through simple chemistry experiments and planting activities that teach by direct observation.

No answers handed over

We set problems so a small group has to work through them together, building understanding instead of memorizing it.

Small-group reasoning

Students work in small groups on set problems, discussing and testing ideas until they arrive at understanding themselves.

A team, not a solo act

Beyond teaching directly, I recruit and direct other tutors so Tareeqa runs as a team rather than one person's effort. That's what makes it possible to hold a fixed, free weekly schedule, and it's why the number of students we reach has grown over time instead of through one-off events.

How we track what's working

Rather than leaning on headcount alone, we log each student's grades before and after every term, and that change feeds directly into how we measure outcomes. The proof of the program's work comes down to three records:

  • Attendance logs that show the weekly schedule was actually kept.
  • Visual lesson plans documenting how each experiment and planting activity was designed.
  • Before and after grades behind every outcome figure we report.

More on how this is compiled is on the Impact page.
