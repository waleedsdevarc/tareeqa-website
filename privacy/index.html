Privacy

What this website collects, why, and how to reach us about it.

The short version

This site is a set of static pages with no sign-up forms, no cookies, no analytics, and no advertising. It doesn't ask you for any personal information, and it doesn't track you across visits.

What this site actually does

  • No forms. There's nowhere on this site to submit your name, email, or any other personal data.
  • No cookies. The site doesn't set any cookies, so there's nothing to consent to.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts. There's no Google Analytics, no pixel, no third-party tracker of any kind.
  • No third-party embeds. Fonts are your device's own system fonts, not loaded from Google Fonts or any other external service. There are no embedded videos, maps, or widgets.
  • No accounts, no logins, nothing to store on our end.

Hosting logs

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub's infrastructure automatically logs standard technical data for every visit, such as IP address, browser type, and request timestamps, as part of running the service. We don't have access to that data ourselves and don't use it for anything. See GitHub's own documentation on data collection for Pages for details on what they log.

The student data mentioned on the Impact page

The attendance and grade records shown on the Impact page come from Tareeqa's own internal tracking, kept separately from this website. Students are identified only by an anonymous ID, never by name, in anything published here.

Questions

If you have a question about this policy or about the site, you can reach the maintainer through GitHub.
